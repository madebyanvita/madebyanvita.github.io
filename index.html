About

I enjoy making sense of complexity.

My work sits where human behaviour, systems thinking, product strategy and interaction design meet, with business impact as the test. Lately that includes creative technology and AI-assisted prototyping.

I care about the whole experience: understanding the problem, working out what matters, making the right calls with engineering, and shipping products that change something measurable. I studied computer science at Pune University, so the engineering side of the table feels familiar.

Over ten years I've designed for consumer products, retail, banking, telecom, e-commerce and enterprise tools.
